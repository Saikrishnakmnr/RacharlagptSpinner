Privacy & your choices

Updated 25 October 2026. This policy describes the supplied version of RacharlaGPT Play at spin.racharlagpt.in.

Play without tracking

Games work without analytics or advertising. Choose “Play privately” on the home page to keep optional Google Analytics off. This is the recommended choice for children. There is no player account, chat, payment form or request for your name.

What stays on your device

Your per-game bests, completed-round count, game list, favorites, daily puzzle records and privacy preference are stored in local storage. They are not a global leaderboard. Clear game data from My scores, or clear this site's browser storage to remove all local preferences. Private browsing, storage restrictions or clearing your browser can remove records. When storage is unavailable, play continues with temporary in-memory progress.

Optional Google Analytics

Adults may choose “I'm 18+ · allow analytics”. Only then does the configured GA4 script load. It can process technical information such as browser and device information, approximate location, page activity and game events, including game IDs and scores. Do not submit personal information through game inputs. We do not send word guesses, names or contact details as game events. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled in our analytics configuration. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

Change your mind

Open Privacy choices in the home-page footer. Choosing Play privately stops future optional analytics after the page reloads and attempts to clear this site's Google Analytics cookies. You can also clear site data in your browser. This does not delete historical records already processed by Google; contact the operator for applicable data requests.

Advertising and sponsorship

Third-party advertising is disabled in this supplied release. A configured paid sponsor may appear in a clearly labeled area outside the games; it uses no external image or script until you visit its link. If Monetag or another network is later enabled, the operator must first update this policy, configure appropriate audience and consent protections, and provide the required provider disclosures. Analytics permission does not grant advertising permission. The legacy advertising push worker is retired by this release. Previously received notifications may need to be cleared in your browser settings.

Hosting and external websites

The hosting provider may process IP addresses, request logs and security data to serve the site. The original project uses GitHub Pages; see GitHub's privacy statement. Hosting practices may change if the operator deploys elsewhere. External sponsor and website links have their own privacy practices.

Children and family use

We do not ask children for personal information through the games. Children should play privately. The optional analytics choice is for adults, not parental consent or an age-verification service. The operator must assess any child-directed audience and applicable consent obligations before adding third-party tracking or advertising.

Contact

Use the operator contact information on the Contact page for privacy requests or questions.
