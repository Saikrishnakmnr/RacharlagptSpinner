About RacharlaGPT Quick Games

RacharlaGPT Quick Games is a small collection of simple browser games created for short, enjoyable breaks. The goal is not competition or complicated rules. It gives visitors a quick way to refresh their attention with a dice roll, a coin toss, a classic Rock Paper Scissors round, or a positive Spin Wheel.

Every game runs directly in your browser using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No account is required to play, and the games are designed to be quick to understand on phones and computers.

Why these games?

Small moments of play can be useful when you need a short pause between study, work, coding, or everyday tasks. Choose a game, play a round, and return to what you were doing. Results are random game outcomes and are provided for entertainment only.

How to Play

🎲 Dice

Press “Roll the Dice” and the game randomly selects a number from one to six. A six gets a special celebration message.

🪙 Coin Toss

Select the clearly marked 🙂 HEADS or 😎 TAILS side, then press “Flip Coin”. The result is randomly selected and compared with your choice.

✊ Rock Paper Scissors

Choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors. The game makes a random bot choice and shows whether you win, lose, or draw.

⚡ Reaction Challenge

Press Start, wait for the panel to turn green, then tap as quickly as possible. Faster reactions earn more points.

🎡 Spin Wheel

Press the Spin button or the wheel itself. The wheel animates through several rotations and lands on one of its positive qualities.
