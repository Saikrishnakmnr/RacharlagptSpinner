How to play Rock Paper Scissors

Pick rock, paper or scissors. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock. Play five rounds against random computer choices. Each win earns 10 points.

Your personal record

The result is measured in points. A higher result is better. Your best is saved on this device and shown in My scores. It is not a verified worldwide ranking, and points cannot be exchanged for money.

A quick, comfortable break

Play on a phone or computer with on-screen controls. Sound is optional. Close or restart the game at any time; an unfinished round does not count as a completed result. No account, payment or download is needed. JavaScript is required to play.
